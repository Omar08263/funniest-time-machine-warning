Entry for the [Funniest Time Machine Warning Label](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-time-machine-warning-f8add151) competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

`main.ts` writes a single warning label (88 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`:

> WARNING: Do not introduce yourself to your mother. She will ground you for thirty years.

The joke turns the stranded-ancestor hazard into the punchline: meet your mother
before you are born and introduce yourself, and the most universal punishment
in the world follows — grounded for thirty years — with the surprise landing in
the last few words, in the straight face of a real safety notice. Deterministic, no network, no randomness, Node 22 standard library only.

Run it with:

```sh
node main.ts
```
